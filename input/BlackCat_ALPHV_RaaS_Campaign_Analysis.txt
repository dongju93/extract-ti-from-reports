==============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

CrowdStrike Intelligence presents a comprehensive analysis of ALPHV (publicly
known as BlackCat), a sophisticated ransomware-as-a-service operation that has
emerged as one of the most technically advanced and operationally active RaaS
families since its emergence in late 2021. ALPHV is the first major ransomware
written entirely in Rust, offering cross-platform capability targeting Windows,
Linux, and VMware ESXi environments.

In the second half of 2023, ALPHV affiliates conducted high-profile attacks
against MGM Resorts International, Caesars Entertainment, and multiple healthcare
organizations, demonstrating the group's willingness to target critical infrastructure
and applying significant pressure through novel extortion tactics including filing
complaints with the SEC against non-disclosing victim companies.

==============================================================================
THREAT ACTOR PROFILE
==============================================================================

ALPHV first advertised on RAMP and XSS dark web forums in November 2021 as
ALPHV/BlackCat. The core development team is assessed to include former members
of DarkSide and BlackMatter RaaS operations (disbanded after the Colonial Pipeline
attack and subsequent law enforcement pressure).

Criminal infrastructure:
  - Tor-based victim negotiation portal
  - Affiliate recruitment portal with ~80% revenue share for affiliates
  - Dedicated data leak site ("ALPHV Collections")
  - Ransomware-as-a-Service builder with highly configurable parameters

Distinctive capabilities:
  1. Rust implementation enables reliable cross-platform ransomware
  2. Configurable encryption modes (AES-128/256, ChaCha20) per deployment
  3. "Intermittent encryption" option for speed (encrypts first N bytes of each file)
  4. Self-propagation module targeting Azure Storage and AWS S3 buckets
  5. Anti-forensics: secure deletion of originals, log wiping

==============================================================================
ALPHV NEGOTIATION TACTICS
==============================================================================

ALPHV employs a three-stage extortion model:
  1. Encryption: Files encrypted, ransom note dropped
  2. Data exposure threat: Victim notified exfiltrated data will be published
  3. Regulatory pressure: For public companies, SEC disclosure complaint filed
     if victim does not respond within 96 hours

The group has also been observed:
  - Contacting victim employees directly with links to stolen HR records
  - Submitting data to competitor companies
  - DDoSing victim infrastructure while negotiating
