Mustang Panda (TA416 / Bronze President): Espionage Campaign Targeting Southeast Asian Governments
Published: 2023-11-14
Source: Trend Micro Research
TLP: WHITE

==============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Trend Micro has identified and investigated a sustained cyber espionage campaign
conducted by Mustang Panda (also tracked as TA416, Bronze President, RedDelta, and
Earth Preta) targeting government ministries, military organizations, and political
parties across Myanmar, Vietnam, the Philippines, and Indonesia.

The campaign, active throughout 2023, leverages geopolitically relevant spear
phishing lures and a newly identified variant of the PlugX remote access Trojan
(RAT), which we designate TONESHELL. Additionally, the threat actor has introduced
a new second-stage backdoor, REDEARTHWORM, which provides more covert long-term
access than PlugX alone.

Mustang Panda is assessed to operate on behalf of Chinese state interests, with a
focus on gathering intelligence related to border disputes, military posture, and
political alliances in the Indochina and Maritime Southeast Asia region.

==============================================================================
BACKGROUND: MUSTANG PANDA
==============================================================================

Mustang Panda has been active since at least 2012. The group primarily targets
government entities in Southeast Asia but has also been observed targeting European
governments (particularly around the Russia-Ukraine conflict), NGOs, and religious
organizations (notably the Vatican and Catholic Church organizations in Asia).

Signature TTPs include:
  - Phishing with ZIP archives containing LNK files and DLL side-loading
  - Heavy use of legitimate software (Correl Draw, HP software, antivirus binaries)
    as hosts for DLL side-loading attacks
  - PlugX RAT as the primary persistent implant
  - Use of USB worms for air-gapped network propagation
  - PANGOLIN rootkit for concealment on critical targets

==============================================================================
CAMPAIGN TECHNICAL DETAILS
==============================================================================

Delivery Mechanism:
Spear phishing emails in local languages (Burmese, Vietnamese, Filipino, Bahasa)
carry ZIP or RAR archives. Archive contents use double-extension filenames to
disguise executables (e.g., "MOU_Agreement_2023.pdf.exe").

The infection chain uses DLL side-loading:
  1. A clean, signed legitimate executable (e.g., HPCustPartUI.exe from HP)
     is bundled with a malicious DLL (HPQBMS.dll or HPQBMT.dll).
  2. When the legitimate binary runs, Windows DLL search order causes it to
     load the malicious DLL from the same directory.
  3. The malicious DLL loads an encrypted shellcode blob from a data file
     (often named with a .dat or .bak extension).

TONESHELL (PlugX Variant):
TONESHELL retains PlugX's modular architecture but adds:
  - Custom protocol over raw TCP (port 443 or 8080, non-standard TLS)
  - Module for USB propagation: copies itself to USB drives and creates
    a hidden autorun configuration targeting Windows AutoPlay
  - Process hollowing to inject into legitimate Windows processes (svchost.exe)

REDEARTHWORM (New Backdoor):
REDEARTHWORM is a lightweight C++ implant with a small footprint (~68KB). It
communicates exclusively over DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 as cover).
Commands are encoded within DNS TXT record queries. Supports: file exfiltration,
command execution, and self-delete. Designed for persistence on air-gapped segments.
