RomCom RAT: Russian-Linked Threat Actor Targets Ukrainian Government and NATO Partners
Published: 2023-11-30
Source: BlackBerry Threat Research and Intelligence
TLP: WHITE

==============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

BlackBerry Threat Research and Intelligence has been tracking RomCom (also
referred to as Storm-0978, Tropical Scorpius, and UNC2596), a Russian-linked
threat actor, throughout 2023. This report focuses on a campaign targeting
Ukrainian government ministries, defense contractors, and NATO member state
organizations providing support to Ukraine.

The campaign demonstrates significant technical evolution compared to earlier
RomCom operations. Key developments include:
  1. A new variant of the RomCom backdoor (version 4.0, dubbed "SINGLE MALT")
     with modular plugin architecture
  2. Use of two CVEs as zero-days prior to vendor patching:
     CVE-2023-36884 (Microsoft Office/Windows HTML RCE)
     CVE-2023-44487 (HTTP/2 Rapid Reset for DDoS-as-distraction)
  3. Enhanced anti-analysis techniques including AMSI patching and ETW disabling

==============================================================================
BACKGROUND: ROMCOM / STORM-0978
==============================================================================

RomCom emerged in 2022 and has since conducted both financially motivated
cybercrime (operating the Underground ransomware) and espionage operations,
exhibiting dual-use characteristics consistent with Russian state-associated
cybercriminal groups. The actor gained attention for:

  - Targeting the 2023 NATO Summit in Vilnius with themed phishing documents
  - Impersonating the Ukrainian World Congress in phishing campaigns
  - Exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities in Microsoft products
  - Operating RomCom RAT, a full-featured remote access Trojan with plugin support
  - Deploying Underground ransomware against select targets post-espionage

RomCom's intelligence collection focus centers on Ukraine military logistics,
Western military aid flows, Ukrainian government communications, and NATO
policy discussions.

==============================================================================
TECHNICAL ANALYSIS
==============================================================================

Delivery Mechanism — Fake NATO Document:
Victims receive emails with subjects such as:
  - "NATO Summit Vilnius 2023 — Restricted Briefing Materials"
  - "NSATU — Northern Support Activities Training Unit Materials"
  - "Ukraine Defense Contact Group — July Session Summary"

Attachments are either DOCX files exploiting CVE-2023-36884 or Windows Shortcut
(.lnk) files delivered inside ZIP archives. The CVE-2023-36884 exploit triggers
when the document is opened in Word, even without enabling macros, by abusing the
Windows Search protocol handler.

RomCom RAT 4.0 "SINGLE MALT":
The backdoor is a 64-bit PE DLL loaded via rundll32.exe with a specific export
ordinal. Architecture:
  - Core module: handles C2 communication, plugin management, persistence
  - Plugin loader: fetches and loads additional modules from C2 on demand
  - Available plugins (observed): keylogger, screenshot, file manager, network
    recon, credential harvester, Mimikatz wrapper, lateral movement (SMB/WMI)

Anti-Analysis:
  AMSI patching: overwrites AmsiScanBuffer() with a RET instruction
  ETW disabling: patches EtwEventWrite() to prevent telemetry
  Parent process spoofing: creates child processes with spoofed parent (explorer.exe)
  Sandbox detection: checks for CPUID hypervisor bit, screen resolution < 800x600,
                     and process count < 30

C2 Protocol:
RomCom 4.0 uses HTTPS with a custom application-layer protocol over WebSocket
(ws:// upgraded to wss://). The operator maintains persistent, low-latency
connections for interactive sessions. All data is encrypted with ChaCha20-Poly1305.
