Earth Lusca Targets Media and Entertainment Organizations with Multi-Stage Backdoor
Published: 2023-09-28
Source: Trend Micro Research
TLP: WHITE

==============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Trend Micro has uncovered a new campaign by Earth Lusca, a China-nexus threat
actor, targeting media companies, entertainment conglomerates, and content
production studios across Hong Kong, Taiwan, South Korea, and the United States.
The campaign, active since June 2023, employs watering hole attacks on industry-
specific news portals and conference websites to deliver a multi-stage infection
chain leading to the MASQLOADER downloader and the SprySOCKS Linux backdoor.

Earth Lusca appears motivated by intellectual property theft — specifically targeting
unreleased content, merger and acquisition discussions, and talent contracts —
suggesting the intelligence collection supports both state and commercial interests.

==============================================================================
BACKGROUND: EARTH LUSCA
==============================================================================

Earth Lusca (also tracked as TAG-22, Charcoal Typhoon, CHROMIUM, ControlX) is
a Chinese-speaking threat actor that Trend Micro began tracking in 2021. The group
targets a wide range of verticals including:

  - Media and entertainment
  - Telecommunications providers
  - Government and political entities (particularly Taiwan-related)
  - Universities and research institutions
  - COVID-19 related organizations (historically)

Earth Lusca shares infrastructure and tooling with APT41 (Winnti Group) and shows
potential overlaps in operator activity, though Trend Micro assesses them as a
distinct cluster. The group is notably sophisticated in its exploitation of internet-
facing applications (Confluence, GitLab, Oracle WebLogic).

==============================================================================
INFECTION CHAIN ANALYSIS
==============================================================================

Stage 1 — Watering Hole:
Earth Lusca compromised four media industry news portals (not named to protect
ongoing investigations). A single malicious JavaScript snippet was injected into
article pages, performing browser fingerprinting (checking for Japanese/Korean/
Taiwanese browser locale settings) before redirecting qualifying visitors to an
exploit landing page.

Stage 2 — Exploit Delivery:
The exploit page serves a drive-by download targeting:
  CVE-2023-3079 (Chrome V8 Type Confusion - before June 2023 patch)
  CVE-2022-4135 (Chrome GPU process heap buffer overflow)

On successful exploitation, a shellcode executes, downloading MASQLOADER.

Stage 3 — MASQLOADER:
MASQLOADER is a .NET-based downloader with an unusual anti-analysis trick: it
requires a specific command-line argument (a 12-character string) to decrypt its
configuration. Without the argument, it produces no output and exits cleanly.
The argument is provided via a scheduled task created during installation.

MASQLOADER downloads the final payload (Cobalt Strike or SprySOCKS) from a
cloud-hosted URL, decrypts it using a hardcoded AES-128 key, and loads it
reflectively into memory.

Stage 4a — Cobalt Strike (Windows targets):
Standard Cobalt Strike beacon used for lateral movement and interactive access
on Windows workstations and servers.

Stage 4b — SprySOCKS (Linux/server targets):
SprySOCKS is a Linux backdoor derived from the open-source Trochilus RAT, adapted
for Linux with a SOCKS5 proxy module. It targets Linux-based content servers,
NAS devices, and development servers common in media production environments.
SprySOCKS communicates via an encrypted TCP protocol unique to this group.
