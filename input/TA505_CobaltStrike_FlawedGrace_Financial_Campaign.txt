TA505 Deploys Cobalt Strike and FlawedGrace RAT Against Financial Services Organizations
Published: 2023-10-19
Source: Proofpoint Threat Research
TLP: WHITE

==============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Proofpoint Threat Research has observed a high-volume email threat campaign
attributed to TA505, a financially motivated threat actor active since at least
2014. The campaign, observed from September through October 2023, targeted financial
institutions, insurance companies, and payment processors in the United States,
United Kingdom, Germany, and Japan.

The attack chain is notable for its use of Microsoft OneNote files (.one) as
initial malware delivery vehicles — a technique TA505 adopted following Microsoft's
disablement of Office macros by default in 2022. The campaign delivers Cobalt Strike
beacons as the initial foothold, followed by deployment of the FlawedGrace Remote
Access Trojan (RAT), TA505's signature implant, for long-term persistence and data
theft.

==============================================================================
BACKGROUND: TA505
==============================================================================

TA505 (also known as CHIMBORAZO, FIN11, and Cl0p) is one of the most prolific
financially motivated threat actors. The group is known for:

  - Operating at massive scale — campaigns regularly exceed 100,000 emails
  - Developing and operating the FlawedAmmyy and FlawedGrace RAT families
  - Association with the Cl0p ransomware operation
  - Heavy use of "get2" and "SDBbot" downloaders
  - Abuse of legitimate file-sharing services (SendGrid, DocuSign-themed lures)

TA505 is assessed to be a Russian-speaking cybercriminal group, likely based in
Eastern Europe, that monetizes access through data theft, fraud, and ransomware.

==============================================================================
CAMPAIGN ANALYSIS
==============================================================================

Email Characteristics:
  Subject lines (representative sample):
    - "Action Required: Invoice #[random 6 digits] attached"
    - "Urgent: Payment confirmation needed - see attached"
    - "ACH Transfer Notice - Please review attachment"
    - "Wire transfer authorization - Reference [random]"

  Volume: approximately 50,000-80,000 messages per campaign wave
  Sender infrastructure: compromised legitimate email accounts and dedicated
  domains registered via NameCheap (1-7 days old at time of sending)

Attachment: Microsoft OneNote (.one)
OneNote files contain embedded image overlays prompting users to click a "VIEW
DOCUMENT" button. The button is actually a linked .hta (HTML Application) file
embedded in the OneNote archive. Clicking executes the HTA via mshta.exe.

The HTA:
  1. Downloads a PowerShell script from a remote host via HTTPS
  2. The PowerShell script deploys a Cobalt Strike beacon (HTTP/HTTPS listener)
  3. The beacon establishes C2 connectivity and downloads FlawedGrace

FlawedGrace RAT:
FlawedGrace (also known as GraceWire) is a .NET-based RAT first documented in 2017.
Key capabilities:
  - Keylogging and screen capture
  - File manager (browse, upload, download, execute)
  - Remote desktop / VNC-like functionality
  - Browser credential harvesting
  - Clipboard monitoring (watches for cryptocurrency addresses, replaces with
    attacker-controlled addresses — clipboard hijacking)
  - Lateral movement via WMI and PsExec
